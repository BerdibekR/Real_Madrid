REAL MADRID EST. 1902

1902 — PRESENT

Built on tradition

Real Madrid was founded in 1902 and quickly became one of the most important football clubs in Spain.

In 1956, Real Madrid won the first European Cup. The club then dominated European football by winning the competition five consecutive times.

From historic victories to generations of legendary players, the club's identity has always been connected with ambition, excellence and winning.
