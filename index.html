MORE THAN A CENTURY OF GLORY

THE HISTORY OF MADRID

Discover the eras that shaped the identity of Real Madrid.

1902
THE BEGINNING

THE FOUNDATION

On 6 March 1902, Madrid Football Club was officially established. This was the beginning of a journey that would change football history.

1950s
THE FIRST EUROPEAN DYNASTY

THE DI STÉFANO ERA

Led by Alfredo Di Stéfano and Ferenc Puskás, Real Madrid dominated European football and won five consecutive European Cups between 1956 and 1960.

1980s
A NEW GENERATION

THE QUINTA DEL BUITRE

A generation of Spanish talents led by Emilio Butragueño brought domestic dominance and unforgettable football.

2000s
THE GALÁCTICOS

THE ERA OF SUPERSTARS

Zidane, Ronaldo Nazário, Beckham, Figo and other world-class footballers brought a new global identity to the club.

2014
THE TENTH EUROPEAN CUP

LA DÉCIMA

Real Madrid defeated Atlético Madrid in Lisbon to win its tenth European Cup. Sergio Ramos' late equaliser became one of football's most famous moments.

2016–18
EUROPEAN DOMINANCE

THE THREE-PEAT

Under Zinedine Zidane, Real Madrid achieved an extraordinary milestone: three consecutive Champions League titles from 2016 to 2018.

2022
ANOTHER HISTORIC NIGHT

THE FOURTEENTH

Real Madrid defeated Liverpool in Paris to claim its fourteenth European Cup, continuing its extraordinary relationship with European football.
